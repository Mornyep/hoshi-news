# STARNEWS · 星闻

[简体中文](README.md) · [繁體中文](README.zh-TW.md) · [English](README.en.md) · [日本語](README.ja.md) · [한국어](README.ko.md)

Bring your questions. Keep the sources in view.

STARNEWS is a mobile-friendly news reader built around your own AI. Write what you want to understand, prepare a sourced question and continue in your official AI client. Past reports keep their sources and dates in the archive. The lime-and-black interface gives headlines, reading and evidence room to breathe.

[Open STARNEWS](https://mornyep.github.io/hoshi-news/) · [Usage and setup](docs/guide.en.md) · [Privacy and limitations](docs/guide.en.md#privacy-and-limitations) · [Updates](docs/guide.en.md#updates)

## Available now

- Free-text reading requirements and local profiles for settings, bookmarks and reading positions.
- Manual handoff to official AI websites: inspect and copy a packet, ask yourself and paste an answer back.
- Historical reports with source links, dates, evidence scope and labelled original-language fallbacks.
- Validated AI presentation suggestions: a fixed palette and up to three exact text highlights, without overriding official warning levels or colors.

The interface supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese and English. Korean is available in documentation only. Pasted answers are not automatically translated or verified.

## Before you start

Local profiles are not authenticated or password-protected accounts. Other people using this browser can select them; use separate browser profiles on shared devices. Switching or exiting clears the page session. Saved local content remains; pasted answers are not persisted.

Subscriptions are not automatically connected. STARNEWS does not read chats or consume a membership allowance. The private backend is undeployed; cross-device sync and nearby disaster notifications are unavailable. Public AI generation has stopped, while historical data keeps its dates.

GitHub Pages publishes the root of `main` without a frontend build. See the [complete guide](docs/guide.en.md) for setup, development checks, deletion and copyright. Media and trademarks belong to their owners. AI summaries may be wrong; linked sources remain essential. The [4.7 technical record](docs/release-4.7.md) is historical.


The first automatic route is a prepared local ChatGPT component using official authorization. The user must complete sign-in; readiness does not mean an account is connected. Other providers remain disabled, and manual handoff is a fallback. See [local connection](docs/local-bridge.md).
